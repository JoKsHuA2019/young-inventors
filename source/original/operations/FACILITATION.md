# Facilitation for all 30 lessons
Use the lesson-specific mentor preparation, five questions, prerequisites and BOM in the app-copy PDF or JSON. Each manuscript includes the same flexible 60-minute schedule with lesson-specific experiments.

0–5: mystery and prediction. 5–10: materials, safety and prior skills. 10–27: build actions and adult checks. 27–37: controlled experiment and observation log. 37–43: explanation grounded in evidence. 43–52: hints or one safe remix. 52–57: private reflection. 57–60: disconnect or stop motion, count parts and clean up.

The schedule is a planning aid, not a speed requirement. Allow a second session for slower work. For the I05 sprint, use the build block for the first prototype and experiment/remix blocks for its evidence-based revision. For I06, use the build block for display preparation and the experiment block for a local presentation.

Pair a builder with an evidence recorder and swap roles. Permit spoken responses, drawings and mentor reading. A learner can direct another person's actions when physical access needs support. Use large-print tables and never depend on wire color or sound alone. Keep later hints concealed until requested when projecting troubleshooting slides.

No physical lesson has an adult sign-off yet. Do not improvise hazardous faults, higher voltages, heavy loads, sharp tools or different boards. Record actual review evidence before use.
