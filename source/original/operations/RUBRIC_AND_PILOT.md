# Private formative rubric and pilot protocol
No public grades, rankings or rewards depend on these scores. Accept spoken, drawn and written evidence equally. Use observations to improve support, not to label children.

| Criterion | 0: not observed yet | 1: with support | 2: independently explained |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prediction | No prediction/uncertainty stated | States guess or uncertainty with prompt | States prediction/uncertainty and a testable reason |
| Safe build/observation | Safe observation not yet shown | Follows cues with mentor | Explains and follows power/motion check routine |
| Evidence | No actual observation recorded | Describes result with prompts | Links repeated observation to a claim |
| Debug/iteration | No next test identified | Chooses suggested safe test | Justifies one-variable test or redesign |
| Tradeoff | Not explained yet | Names one benefit/cost with help | Uses evidence to explain benefit and cost/limitation |
| Creative extension | Not suggested yet | Chooses a remix | Proposes safe extension and success criterion |

A correctly guessed prediction is not worth more than a revised one. “Still not working” with an honest observation and a justified safe next test is a valuable outcome. Completing intellectual work requires a prediction/uncertainty, an observation and an explanation or iteration; copying a build alone is insufficient.

## Two-learner pilot after adult physical review
Use one newer and one more experienced learner; guardian permission for the activity as appropriate, no public media. Explain that the lesson is being tested, not the child. Allow stop/skip and flexible pacing. Record anonymous session codes only if needed; a no-record oral debrief is acceptable. Record support requested, confusing steps, what the learner predicted, their observation and their own design choice. Ask “What made you curious?” and “When did you decide something yourself?”

Measure the blueprint's discovery and understanding targets only in a later app pilot; they are goals, not results. This pack has no participant data, pilot success rate or completed child usability test. Have the Foundation decide any data retention before collecting identifiable material.

## Suggested workshop routes (after content and physical review)
- First light: C01 then C04 on another session; use I02 only after polarity readiness.
- No electronics: M01; then M02 and I03 after physical review.
- Code input: facilitator-taught P01 readiness, then P03.
- Sensor inquiry: facilitator-taught P01 readiness, then S01; S02 next after physical review.
- Invention: mechanical I01 entry → I04 after physical review, then reviewed kit-based I05 → local I06.
These are recommended sequences, not locks. All 30 lessons now have full manuscripts. Physical review is still required before use.
