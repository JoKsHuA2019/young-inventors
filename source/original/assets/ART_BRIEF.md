# Original illustration assets
Ten original six-scene sheets use paper collage and colored-pencil texture. For each track, the story sheet shows the project idea and the action sheet shows making or trying. The artwork does not provide exact wiring or prove a tested build.

`child_pages.json` supplies the sheet path and cell (0–2 top row; 3–5 bottom row). PNG masters and full-size JPEG versions are retained. `slidesheets` contains smaller JPEG copies for dependable PowerPoint import. Existing connection maps and SVGs are technical helper references only.

No images, characters, passages or distinctive rhyming patterns from published children’s books are included. The book research informed short read-aloud sentences, concrete scenes, page turns and generous picture space. Publisher and author references are listed in the helper guide.
