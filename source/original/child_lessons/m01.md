# The cardboard jaw

This cardboard jaw
has a long, long tail.
Push the tail down.
What could rise?

---

Where would you push
to lift the same paper load
more easily?

---

Your helper fits the turning point.
It is called a pivot.
Tape on a tiny paper load.
Find the two marked push spots.

---

Push gently at the near spot.
Then try the far spot.
Lift to the same mark each time.
Keep fingers away from the pivot.

---

The far spot can feel easier.
But your hand moves farther
to lift the load
the same height.

---

Turn your lever into an animal.
What moves when you push?
Show someone the easier push spot.
