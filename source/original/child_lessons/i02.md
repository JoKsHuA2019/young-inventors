# The light is out

The little light
will not shine.
Welcome to
the repair shop.

---

Could a loose wire
and a backwards LED
both make the light go dark?

---

Your helper has hidden
one safe problem in a checked circuit.
Unplug the battery.
Look before changing anything.

---

Choose one thing to check.
Let your helper check the change.
Then try power.
Unplug before trying another fix.

---

One small change
can give us a useful clue.
Changing everything at once
hides which part helped.

---

Tell your repair story.
What did you check?
What happened?
What would you try next?
