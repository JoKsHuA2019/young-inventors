# The little traffic light

Red. Green. Yellow.
Our tiny town
needs a traffic light.

---

If green stays on longer,
will the whole pattern
take longer too?

---

Keep USB unplugged.
Build the three lights
with your helper.
Each light gets its own resistor.

---

Ask your helper to check and upload.
Watch one whole pattern.
Now make only the green wait
twice as long.
Watch again.

---

The order can stay the same
while the timing changes.
A longer green wait
makes this whole pattern longer.

---

How long should red last
in your pretend town?
Choose a time, try it,
and tell someone what changed.
