# Wait for the light

Your finger is ready.
The light is not.
Wait...
Wait...

---

If you press before the light,
should that try count?

---

Unplug USB.
Build the button and light
with your helper.
Ask them to check and upload the game.

---

Let go of the button.
Wait for the light.
Then press!
Try an early press once too.
What does the game say?

---

The game checks
when you press.
An early press is a false start.
A press after the light gives a time.

---

Try five fair turns.
What changes as you practise?
Your helper can explain
why the timing is only an estimate.
