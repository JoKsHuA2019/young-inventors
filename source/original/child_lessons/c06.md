# A glowing picture

A house in the woods.
A star in the sky.
What picture could you make
with one little light?

---

Where should your light go
so someone can see
what you made?

---

Draw your picture first.
Keep the battery unplugged
while you and your helper
fit the little light and wires.

---

Your helper checks the wires.
Turn on the light.
Move the picture gently
to its display spot.
Does it still work?

---

Paper can tug on a wire.
A wire can come loose.
Unplug before fixing it.
Then check and try again.

---

Change one part of your picture.
What looks better now?
Show someone what you changed.
