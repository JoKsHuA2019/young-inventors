# An echo in a box

Hello, cardboard wall!
The sensor sends out sound.
Will an echo
come back?

---

Will a closer wall
send the echo back
sooner?

---

Your helper builds and checks
the sound sensor.
Hold a flat cardboard target
facing its two round openings.

---

Try about 10, 20 and 40 centimetres.
Read three numbers at each spot.
If no reading comes back,
write “missing,” not zero.

---

Sound goes to the wall
and back again.
A longer trip
usually takes more time.

---

Try a soft fabric target
at the same safe distance.
Does it give the same clear echo?
