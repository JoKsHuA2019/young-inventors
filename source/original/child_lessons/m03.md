# A leaf that waves

Our paper leaf
wants to wave hello.
Can a little motor help?

---

Will two different commands
make the leaf point
two different ways?

---

This small motor is a servo.
Your helper sets up its own power
and checks it without the leaf.
Keep hands clear.

---

Turn both power sources off.
Your helper fits the light paper leaf.
After another check, watch it move
between the two safe positions.

---

The board sends a signal.
The servo tries to turn
to the position we asked for.

---

Change a pause in the code
with your helper.
Can your leaf wave slowly?
Keep the same safe positions.
