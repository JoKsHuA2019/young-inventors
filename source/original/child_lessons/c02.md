# The secret flap

This flap has a secret.
Close it,
and a little light can shine.

---

What do you think happens
when the shiny foil pieces
stop touching?

---

Unplug the battery.
Make the flap with your helper.
The two foil pieces must meet
when the flap closes.

---

Ask your helper to check.
Connect the battery.
Open the flap. Close it.
Open it again.
What does the light do?

---

Touching foil closes the path.
The light comes on.
A gap opens the path.
The light goes off.

---

Can you make a paper door
that works the same way?
Tell someone where the path breaks.
