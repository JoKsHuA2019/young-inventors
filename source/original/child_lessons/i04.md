# Two handles

One paper flap.
Two handles.
Which would you
like to use?

---

Would the same handle
feel easiest
to everyone?

---

Imagine someone using your flap.
Would they pull it or push it?
Make two paper handles
with your helper.

---

Ask a partner to try them
in a comfortable way.
Listen to what they say.
Change one handle and try again.

---

A bigger grip
might help one person.
Another person
might choose a different shape.

---

Show both versions.
What did your partner
help you notice?
