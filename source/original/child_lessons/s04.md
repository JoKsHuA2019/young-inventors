# Getting closer

Our toy block
is rolling toward the wall.
Can a little light
give us a clue?

---

As the block gets closer,
should the flashes get
faster or slower?

---

Your helper checks
the sensor and flashing light.
This is a tabletop model,
not a safety device.

---

Try the target at
15, 35 and 70 centimetres.
Count flashes for the same time.
Then remove the target.
What does the screen say?

---

Closer readings make faster flashes.
A missing echo means
we do not know the distance.
An off light cannot mean “all clear.”

---

Can you invent a different sign
for “I do not know”?
Tell someone why
your model needs it.
