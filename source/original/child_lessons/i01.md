# What is in the box?

There is something
inside this box.
All we can see
is a little tab.

---

If you push the tab
and let go,
will it come back?

---

Your helper gives you
a safe cardboard mystery box.
Look at the outside first.
Leave the inside closed.

---

Push gently and let go.
Try the same move again.
What did you see?
What might be making it happen?

---

“The tab came back”
is something we saw.
“An elastic band pulled it”
is one possible reason.

---

Think of another reason.
What gentle test could help?
Ask before opening the box.
