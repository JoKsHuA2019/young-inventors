# A secret message

Two short flashes.
One long flash.
Someone at the pretend camp
is sending a message.

---

Could you tell two messages apart
just by watching
how long the light stays on?

---

Choose a silent light pattern
with your helper.
A quiet beep works too.
Let your helper check the sound first.

---

Try one short signal
and one long signal.
Keep the sound comfortable,
or use only light.
Ask someone to copy the pattern.

---

A signal can be short or long.
Our light uses that length
to make a message.

---

Make two secret messages.
Can a partner tell them apart
three times in a row?
