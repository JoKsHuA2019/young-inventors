# A light for the tent

The tent has a door.
The tent has a bed.
Now it needs a light.

---

Can a light shine
if there is a gap
in one of its wires?

---

Your helper adds a resistor.
It helps protect the little light.
With the battery unplugged,
build the wire loop together.

---

Your helper checks the loop.
Plug in the battery.
Does the light shine?
Unplug before your helper opens one wire.
Check together, then try again.

---

A gap breaks the path.
Our light goes out.
Unplug before closing the gap.
Check the loop and try once more.

---

Where will your light go?
In a cave?
In a tiny house?
Tell someone what made it go dark.
