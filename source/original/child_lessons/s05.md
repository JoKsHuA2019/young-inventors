# A warm little bead

Here is a tiny bead.
Your hand is warm.
Could the bead
notice?

---

What might happen
to the number
when the bead gets warmer?

---

Your helper checks
the insulated bead and its wires.
Touch only the dry, covered bead.
Keep fingers off the metal legs.

---

Watch the room readings.
Gently warm the checked bead
with dry fingertips.
Let go and wait.
Watch the numbers again.

---

The numbers may change slowly.
They are clues about this bead.
They are not yet
a temperature in degrees.

---

Draw the way
your numbers changed.
If the change was too small to tell,
say so.
