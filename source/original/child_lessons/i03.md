# Copy the move

This machine makes
a lovely little move.
Could yours do that
with different cardboard parts?

---

If one link gets longer,
will the end
move the same way?

---

Watch the cardboard model move.
Draw what it does.
Your helper fits your paper pins
and checks that the parts turn freely.

---

Try the same small handle move
three times.
Stop before moving one paper strip
or its turning point.
Try again. What changed?

---

Different parts
can make similar moves.
But one version may wobble
more than the other.

---

Choose one thing
you want to make better.
Try one change.
Did it help?
