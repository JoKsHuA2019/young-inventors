# A light for the dark

The little reading nook
is getting dark.
Can its lamp
turn on by itself?

---

How could the board decide
when it is
dark enough?

---

Your helper checks the sensor and LED.
Read the numbers in light and shade.
Together, choose a number
between those two groups.

---

Try the light rule.
Cover the sensor.
Uncover it.
What happens to the lamp?
Now try a different room.

---

That number tells our code
when to turn the lamp on.
In another room,
we may need a new number.

---

Make a paper shade
for your reading nook.
Did the lamp ever turn on
when you did not expect it?
