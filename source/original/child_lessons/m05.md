# Up goes the bridge

A paper boat is coming.
The bridge is in the way.
Up, little bridge!

---

Could a button
lift the bridge
without your hand touching it?

---

Your helper checks the servo first.
With both power sources off,
fit the light bridge together.
Leave the hinge clear.

---

After your helper checks,
press and let go.
Watch the bridge open and close.
Stop if it catches or strains.

---

The button sends a request.
The code picks a position.
The servo moves the bridge.

---

Make a paper boat
that fits your river.
What would you check
if the bridge got stuck?
