# The drawing machine

A handle turns.
The marker moves.
A line grows
across the paper.

---

If we move one pivot,
will the line
follow the same path?

---

Your helper fits the paper pins.
Tape on a light marker.
The parts must turn freely.
Never force them.

---

Turn the handle through
the same small move three times.
Stop. Your helper moves one pivot.
Try the same move again.

---

The links guide each other.
A new pivot spot
can make a new path.

---

Turn your lines into a picture.
Which pivot did you move?
Tell someone how the line changed.
