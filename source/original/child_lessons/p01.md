# Blink, little light

The board has a tiny light.
On. Off.
On. Off.
Can we change its blink?

---

What will happen
if the light waits
for less time?

---

Your helper opens the code.
Find both waits marked 500.
Watch the first blink pattern
before changing anything.

---

Change both waits to 250.
Ask your helper to upload.
Watch the same little light.
Is it faster now?

---

The code repeats.
Turn on. Wait.
Turn off. Wait.
Shorter waits make faster blinks.

---
