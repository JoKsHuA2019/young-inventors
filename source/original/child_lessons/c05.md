# Two bright windows

Our little house
has two windows.
We want a light
in each one.

---

If one light loses its path,
must the other go dark too?

---

Unplug the battery.
Build two paths with your helper.
Each light needs
its own resistor.

---

Check together, then connect power.
Look at both lights.
Unplug before your helper opens one path.
Check and try again.
Does the other light stay on?

---

Each light has its own path.
One path can break
while the other stays whole.

---

Make two rooms for your lights.
Show someone each path.
What do both paths share?
