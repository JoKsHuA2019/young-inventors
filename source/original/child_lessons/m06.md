# A creature with a wiggle

This paper creature
has something to say.
No words.
Just a wiggle.

---

Could a slow move
and a quick move
look like different moods?

---

Your helper checks the servo
with nothing attached.
Turn both power sources off
before fitting your light paper creature.

---

After your helper checks,
watch the two moves.
Change one pause in the code.
Keep the same safe positions.

---

Timing can change
how a move looks.
Your partner might see excitement.
You might see surprise.

---

Give your creature a greeting.
What did your partner think
it was saying?
