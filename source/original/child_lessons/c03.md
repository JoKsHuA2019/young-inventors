# The paperclip test

Here is a paperclip.
Here is a plastic lid.
They look different.
Will they do different things?

---

Which one could help
our little light shine?
What is your guess?

---

Your helper makes a safe tester.
It has one gap.
With the battery unplugged,
put one clean, dry object in the gap.

---

Ask your helper to check.
Connect the battery and look.
Unplug before trying the next object.
Try four objects.
Which ones make the light shine?

---

Metal lets electricity pass.
It is a conductor.
Plastic often does not.
Check for loose wires
before deciding.

---

Choose a safe mystery object
for your helper to test.
What made you think
it would work?
