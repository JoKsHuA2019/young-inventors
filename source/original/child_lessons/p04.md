# A softer glow

One light.
Two different glows.
How can we make
the light look dimmer?

---

Which number will look brighter:
64 or 192?

---

Unplug the board.
Build the light and resistor
with your helper.
Let them choose the right connection.

---

Ask your helper to check and upload.
Try 64.
Then try 192.
Watch from the same place.
Which glow looks brighter?

---

The light flashes too fast to see.
More time on
usually looks brighter.
Less time on looks dimmer.

---

Can you make a slow, soft glow
for a paper lighthouse?
Tell someone what the number changes.
