# I made that!

Look what you made!
A little house.
A wiggly creature.
Something that began as an idea.

---

What could you show
to help someone understand
how it works?

---

Ask your helper to check your model.
You can show a drawing instead.
Find your notes.
Keep names and private details off the display.

---

Tell someone:
“This was my first idea.
This is what happened.
This is what I changed.”

---

A story about a mistake
can help explain
a better idea.

---

Ask your listener
what they noticed.
What would you like
to make next?
