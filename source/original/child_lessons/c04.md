# Turn it around

An LED is a tiny light.
This one has two legs.
Does it matter
which way they go?

---

If we turn the LED around,
will it still shine?

---

Unplug the battery.
Ask your helper to mark the legs.
Build the loop together.
Keep the resistor in its place.

---

Check together, then try it.
Unplug.
Your helper turns only the LED around.
Check again before connecting power.
What changed?

---

An LED has a direction.
In our loop, one way shines.
The other way stays dark.
The resistor stays in place.

---

Make a little paper label
to show the right way round.
How could you help someone
remember it?
