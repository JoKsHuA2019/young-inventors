# A paper cloud

A paper cloud
passes over the sensor.
Will the board
notice the shade?

---

Will the number get bigger
or smaller
when you cover the sensor?

---

Your helper builds and checks
the light-sensor circuit.
Ask them to show you
the changing numbers.

---

Watch three numbers in room light.
Shade the sensor with dry card.
Watch three more.
Take the card away.
What happens now?

---

For our circuit,
brighter light usually gives
a bigger number.
Shade usually gives a smaller one.

---

Make a paper cloud
that can slide past the sensor.
Tell someone what happened
to the numbers.
