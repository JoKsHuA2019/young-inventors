# Turn the dial

A little turn here.
A little move there.
Can your dial
move a paper puppet?

---

What will the puppet do
when you turn the dial
toward its other end?

---

Your helper sets up
the dial and servo.
The servo keeps its own power supply.
All wiring changes happen with power off.

---

Turn slowly.
Try the low end,
the middle and the high end.
Keep clear of the moving arm.

---

The dial changes a reading.
The code uses that reading
to choose a servo position.

---

Give your puppet a job.
Can it point to three places?
Show someone how the dial
controls the move.
