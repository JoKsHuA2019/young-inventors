# The open box

The treasure box opens.
Light slips inside.
Can our sensor
spot the change?

---

Would opening the box
always make it brighter
inside?

---

Your helper checks
the light sensor and lamp.
Keep wires clear of the lid.
Compare open and closed readings.

---

Try five openings
in one room.
Use the same rule
in a darker room.
Does it still notice every opening?

---

The sensor follows light,
not the lid itself.
A dark room
can hide the clue.

---

Move the sensor
with power off and your helper’s help.
Check and try again.
Did the new spot work better?
